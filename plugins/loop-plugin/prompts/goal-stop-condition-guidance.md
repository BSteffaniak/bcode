Stop condition guidance: Cover the complete requested outcome using current inspectable evidence for each requirement. The evaluator is read-only: inspect existing results and state; do not require it to mutate files or perform implementation. Missing or indirect verification means unverified, not achieved. Partial completion, repeated claims, a blocker, resource exhaustion, or the implementing agent saying done are not success. Require applicable project validation evidence without inventing additional deliverables. For subjective criteria identify the needed evidence or explicit user acceptance rather than fabricating an automatic oracle.
